# Brainstorming for Brickout Revenge Game Development

## Summary

- Concept of the game is "Brickout Revenge," where players control bricks against a paddle and balls.
- Game design inspired by Age of Empires II and Handmaid Hero, focusing on data-oriented coding.
- Standard mobile game layout: 4 bricks wide and 5 bricks high, with scrolling levels.
- Features a level editor for players to customize ball order and economic conditions.
- Premium membership model proposed at $10/month or $60/year, offering enhanced gameplay options.
- Emphasis on a fun, math-focused, and data-driven experience with a solid economy system.
- Plans for in-game progression, tournaments, and a tech tree, with careful balancing of brick and ball types.
- Minimal ad presence, focusing on time and level restrictions for free users.

## Detailed Notes

- Game Concept: Brickout Revenge involves players as bricks against a paddle and balls. The design aims for a data-oriented approach, inspired by Age of Empires II and Handmaid Hero.
- Layout: Standard mobile game dimensions will be 4 bricks wide and 5 bricks high, with levels allowing for scrolling.
- Level Editor: Players can customize the order of incoming balls and adjust economic conditions to create varied gameplay experiences.
- Animation and Platforms: The game will utilize SVG-based animations and aim for compatibility across multiple platforms.
- User Interface:
  - A menu will allow players to choose between a main campaign and custom games.
  - Premium membership options will enhance gameplay, allowing for more extensive level editing and game access.
- Monetization:
  - Premium membership priced at approximately $10/month or $60/year.
  - Free users may have limited access (for example, 2 games per day, 20 minutes each) with potential ad-based unlocks.
- Game Mechanics:
  - Focus on fun, math-driven gameplay with a solid economy based on bricks.
  - Two main resources: scrap and power, used for various upgrades and actions.
  - Balancing will involve different brick and ball types, with bonuses and penalties based on interactions.
- Progression:
  - In-game progression will be meaningful, with choices affecting gameplay paths.
  - Tech tree mechanics will allow for upgrades that benefit multiple units or specific types.
- Gameplay Dynamics:
  - Players can choose to build a strong economy before focusing on defense or offense.
  - Level editor will operate on a frame-based system, allowing for strategic decision-making.
- Testing and Development:
  - The game will be developed using the Stasis language, with an emphasis on quick iteration and testing across platforms.
